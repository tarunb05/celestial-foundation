( Our mission )

Small acts. Big smiles. We show up at government schools and communities across Bangalore — sharing stationery, donating technology, breaking the silence around periods and running camps where smiles grow and memories remain.

01

Education for all

Stationery drives, learning camps and classroom support for children in government schools.

02

Health & dignity

Menstrual awareness sessions and myth-busting campaigns that normalise conversations about periods.

03

Digital empowerment

Getting technology into classrooms so every child can build the skills to thrive in a digital world.
